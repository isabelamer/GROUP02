Conference Support System — Relational Model

conference (conference_id, name [NN], acronym [NN], description [NN])

conference_edition (edition_id, #conference_id → conference [NN], year [NN], start_date [NN], end_date [NN], location [NN]) {UK: conference_id, year} {CK: end_date >= start_date}

participant (participant_id, name [NN], email [NN] [UK], affiliation [NN])

edition_participant (#edition_id → conference_edition, #participant_id → participant)

activity (activity_id, #edition_id → conference_edition [NN], title [NN], description [NN], start_time [NN], end_time [NN]) {CK: end_time > start_time}

involvement (#activity_id → activity, #participant_id → participant, role [NN]) {CK: role IN ('speaker', 'chair', 'attendee')}

paper (paper_id, #edition_id → conference_edition [NN], title [NN], abstract [NN], submission_date [NN], status [NN], decision, decision_date) {CK: status IN ('submitted', 'under_review', 'withdrawn')} {CK: decision IN ('accepted', 'rejected')} {CK: (decision IS NULL) = (decision_date IS NULL)} {CK: decision_date >= submission_date}

authorship (#paper_id → paper, #participant_id → participant, position [NN]) {UK: paper_id, position} {CK: position >= 1}

review (#paper_id → paper, #reviewer_id → participant, score [NN], assessment [NN], recommendation [NN], submission_date [NN]) {CK: score BETWEEN 1 AND 5} {CK: recommendation IN ('accept', 'weak_accept', 'weak_reject', 'reject')}

proceedings (proceedings_id, #edition_id → conference_edition [NN], title [NN], publication_date [NN], publication_identifier [NN] [UK])

proceedings_editor (#proceedings_id → proceedings, #participant_id → participant)

published_contribution (contribution_id, #proceedings_id → proceedings [NN], #paper_id → paper [UK], title [NN], first_page [NN], last_page [NN], doi [UK]) {CK: first_page >= 1} {CK: last_page >= first_page}

contribution_author (#contribution_id → published_contribution, #participant_id → participant, position [NN]) {UK: contribution_id, position} {CK: position >= 1}

Constraints enforced by triggers

  • An activity's start and end must fall within the dates of its conference_edition (the end may be at any time of the edition's last day). The dates of an edition cannot be changed so as to exclude its existing activities.
  • A participant involved in an activity must participate in the conference_edition of that activity (edition_participant). This is checked when an involvement is inserted or updated, when an activity changes edition, and when a participation is removed.
  • A paper linked to a published_contribution must have decision = 'accepted' and belong to the same conference_edition as the proceedings of that contribution. A published paper cannot change its decision or edition, and the proceedings cannot change edition.
  • A participant cannot review a paper they authored, and cannot become an author of a paper they reviewed.

Constraints not enforced by the relational schema

  • Page ranges of published contributions in the same proceedings must not overlap.
  • A review's submission_date must not precede the submission_date of its paper.
  • Every paper and every published contribution has at least one author; every proceedings has at least one published contribution and at least one editor.
